## Hero generation specification

Mode: built-in image generation, not the API/CLI fallback. No reference image or identifiable person was used.

Prompt brief: Create original editorial 3D artwork for a marketing, creative strategy and social media portfolio. Represent a thought becoming a conversation using a continuous sculptural ribbon, forming an airy open speech-loop with a trailing tail. Use satin cobalt on one side, pale lilac on the reverse, softly frosted edges and the thickness and folds of premium paper. Compose a diagonal floating sculpture within the central 75% of a portrait 4:5 frame. Use a seamless pale cool lilac studio background, soft contact shadow and upper-left studio light. No lettering, logos, people, interface, charts, phones or sparkles. This is decorative website identity, not evidence of a client campaign.

The selected output was converted to WebP for delivery without changing its depicted content. The full-resolution generated PNG remains in the chat's generated-image output; the website uses the self-contained local WebP above.
