## Domain and positioning

Akruti is an early-career marketing and communication practitioner studying MA Mass Communication at XIM University, specialising in Advertising and Marketing Communication. Her strongest supported overlap is audience understanding, social content planning, copy and design, and campaign delivery. Photography, campus publishing, event coordination, and partner outreach add practical range. Present her as a marketer who can make things, with a growing interest in creator partnerships.

The portfolio should answer: Who was the audience? What was the communication task? What did Akruti personally contribute? What was produced? What can be substantiated about the outcome?

## Editorial decisions

1. Lead with ALLEN as the clearest result-backed story, then campus communication, AiGENThix, Usha retail marketing and independent video production.
2. Keep the 4.2M+ figure attached to its two-page/two-month scope and date, with the source identified in the case study.
3. Describe influencer marketing as an area she wants to work in; no invented creators, budgets, deliverables, or results.
4. Only show academic projects after their actual outputs are available. The Claude artifact names Nakhrey and several analyses but supplies no original files.
5. Use newly designed typographic project covers as portfolio navigation, not representations of delivered client campaign assets. Label their status where displayed in case studies.
6. Use an original generated sculpture for the website identity. Never present generated photography, campaign screenshots, analytics, or testimonials as her past work.
7. Keep one direct email action, a copy-email fallback, the supplied résumé, and source links. No dead buttons or pretend form submissions.
